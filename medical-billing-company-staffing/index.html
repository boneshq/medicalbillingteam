Add capacity where your operating plan needs it

A new client can create more work before you can justify another local hire. A vacancy can leave follow-up queues unattended. A growing billing company may also need its experienced US staff to spend more time on client communication, quality review and complex exceptions.

Describe that operating problem in the brief. Avoid starting with an arbitrary number of billers and hoping the workload fits. The AGS medical billing service includes dedicated back-office staffing for billing companies; the assessment determines the proposed roles and responsibilities for your business.

Keep ownership of your client process

Before onboarding, put the handoffs in writing. A useful ownership map has three columns: the work assigned to the dedicated staff, decisions your supervisor retains, and issues that go to the AGS contact.

Define thisPut it in the operating brief
Queue ownershipWhich accounts, payers and tasks are included, and which are excluded
Decision authorityWho can change codes, approve appeals, adjust balances or authorize write-offs
Client communicationWho speaks for your billing company and which issues require your approval
Quality reviewWho checks samples, how errors are corrected and when work is escalated
AccessApproved systems, individual permissions and the process for removing access

If you require confidentiality, restrictions on contacting your clients, or a particular branding arrangement, make those explicit contract requirements. Do not assume that a general staffing agreement includes them. Our RFP template and vendor scorecard provides a reusable way to record the scope and compare evidence.

Budget the team, not just an hourly rate

Use the outsourced staffing pricing estimator to work through roles, hours and headcount. Then add costs your company retains, such as software seats, access setup, training and supervisory time. A staff-hour estimate does not tell you how many claims will be paid.

For each proposed role, ask:

  • What work will this person own during the agreed hours?
  • What training and review time will our current staff need to provide?
  • Which charges are included in the quote, and which are separate?
  • What happens during leave, turnover or a change in workload?
  • What term, notice period and exit obligations apply?

Compare that scope with your in-house alternative using the staffing cost comparison. Keep service fees, employee wages and percentage-of-collections charges separate so the comparison remains meaningful.

Plan access before production work begins

AGS states that it signs a BAA before staff access PHI. Your organization should also review its own client agreements and proposed subcontracting arrangement. HHS explains that business associates and relevant subcontractors have agreement obligations when they handle PHI. HHS business associate guidance.

Use aggregate counts and fictional examples during initial scoping. Any later access to production information belongs in your approved systems and authorization process. The outsourced billing HIPAA guide lists questions to bring to your compliance lead.

Give the first assignment a measurable starting point

Record the opening queue, incoming work, completed actions, blocked items and quality-review method. Keep those definitions consistent between reporting periods. Reconcile changes in backlog before attributing them to staff performance; new arrivals, payer responses and practice documentation all affect the result.

A proposed rollout can use three checkpoints: agreement on scope, completion of access and training, and a review of actual work against the agreed definitions. Discuss timing with AGS; this is a planning framework, not a fixed onboarding schedule.

FAQ

Can Alliance Global Solutions staff a medical billing company?

Yes. AGS provides dedicated staff for medical billing companies as well as practices and treatment centers. Tell us which work you want to assign, your systems, coverage hours and supervision needs so the team can confirm a suitable scope.

Who manages the outsourced staff's daily work?

AGS hires and monitors the staff. Your billing company trains them on its systems, payers and rules and directs the work. Agree on the responsibility for quality review, exceptions and escalation before starting.

Can we use our existing billing systems?

The dedicated staffing model is built around your workflows. Identify the systems, licenses, permissions and training required during scoping. Confirm that your contracts and access policies permit the proposed arrangement before granting access.

Can we start with a specific A/R or denial queue?

Bring the queue, approximate volume, age bands and required coverage to the assessment. AGS can discuss the staffing scope, candidate needs and engagement terms. A defined queue is useful for scoping, but it is not a promise of a particular minimum team or short-term contract.

Will outsourcing guarantee higher collections?

No. Staffing adds capacity for agreed work. Collections also depend on payer decisions, documentation, contract terms and your operating processes. Evaluate completed work and quality alongside payment results using consistent definitions.
