How the model works

Alliance Global Solutions places dedicated employees with each client. They aren't a shared pool that rotates between accounts. The company handles hiring and monitoring; the client trains the staff on its own payers, software and rules, then directs the day-to-day work. That keeps the client in control of the process and its payer relationships, which is the main difference from a full-service billing company. See medical billing outsourcing: three models for how this compares.

Team size depends on the client, from 2-3 dedicated staff to 50+. A small outpatient center might start with a few people on verification and follow-up; a billing company serving many practices might use a larger team across several queues, and Alliance Global Solutions handles a lot of back-end work for clients like that. Some client relationships have lasted five to six years.

Getting started in 3 steps

  1. Tell us what you need. Request help with practice-level details only, never patient information. Your request may be referred to Alliance Global Solutions.
  2. Scope the team and sign the BAA. Agree on roles and team size, review pricing in writing, and sign a business associate agreement before anyone accesses PHI.
  3. Interview, train and start. Interview your dedicated staff, set up access in your systems and train them on your payers and workflows. Alliance Global Solutions handles hiring and monitoring.

How does the Alliance Global Solutions staffing model work?

Alliance Global Solutions recruits, hires and monitors its staff, and assigns dedicated employees to each client rather than a shared pool. Clients interview their staff, train them on their own payers, software and rules, and direct the day-to-day work.
