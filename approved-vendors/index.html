Alliance Global Solutions

Alliance Global Solutions provides dedicated remote staff for medical billing and revenue cycle work, with US-based management and admin and staff in the Philippines, Colombia and other countries. It handles hiring and monitoring of staff, and its clients train those staff on their own workflows. Its clients range from small outpatient centers to large billing companies that handle billing for multiple clients, with teams from 2-3 dedicated staff to 50+, and it handles a lot of their back-end work. It specializes in medical billing, including verification of benefits (VOB) for drug and alcohol treatment centers and broader revenue cycle work. Alliance Global Solutions signs a business associate agreement (BAA) with each client before any of its staff access protected health information. Technology

Most vendors now combine staff with automation for eligibility checks, claim status and appeal drafting. When you compare vendors, ask which tools they use, who reviews AI output and whether every tool vendor that touches PHI signs a BAA (HHS).
