How to use it

Answer each item Yes, No or Not sure. Every "No" or "Not sure" is a place to look first. This is a self-assessment, not an audit or legal review.

1. Front end: scheduling, eligibility and authorizations

  • ☐ We verify eligibility and benefits before every visit, not just for new patients.
  • ☐ We re-check insurance cards and demographics at every visit.
  • ☐ We identify coordination of benefits (which plan is primary) before the visit.
  • ☐ We maintain a current prior authorization matrix by payer and service.
  • ☐ We track authorization expirations and visit limits.
  • ☐ We give the front desk an estimated patient responsibility to collect at check-in.
  • ☐ We identify behavioral health carve-outs during eligibility checks (if applicable).

2. Charges and claims

  • ☐ Charges are entered within 1-2 business days of the date of service.
  • ☐ Claims are scrubbed before submission.
  • ☐ Clearinghouse and payer rejections are worked daily.
  • ☐ We track timely filing limits for each major payer.
  • ☐ Coding is done or reviewed by credentialed coders for our specialty.
  • ☐ We updated our systems for this year's code changes (ICD-10-CM changes Oct 1; CPT/HCPCS changes Jan 1, plus quarterly updates).

3. Denials

  • ☐ Every denial lands in one queue with a named owner.
  • ☐ Denials are categorized by payer and reason.
  • ☐ Denials are worked at least weekly, by deadline and dollar value.
  • ☐ Appeals are tracked with deadlines and outcomes.
  • ☐ Top preventable denial reasons are reviewed monthly and fed back to the front desk and coders.

4. Accounts receivable

  • ☐ We calculate days in A/R the same way every month.
  • ☐ We review A/R by aging bucket and by payer monthly.
  • ☐ A/R follow-up is prioritized by dollar value and age, not alphabetically.
  • ☐ Every touched claim has a documented action and next follow-up date.
  • ☐ We have a written write-off policy with approval levels.
  • ☐ Patient A/R has its own workflow (statements, reminders, payment plans).

5. Payment posting

  • ☐ ERAs are posted daily and reconciled to bank deposits.
  • ☐ Zero-pay and partial-pay lines are flagged as denials.
  • ☐ We compare payments to contracted rates to catch underpayments.
  • ☐ Secondary claims go out promptly after primary payment.
  • ☐ Credit balances are reviewed and resolved regularly.

6. Reporting

  • ☐ Leadership sees a monthly KPI report (see worksheet below).
  • ☐ We can see each staff member's queue and output.
  • ☐ We know our top 5 payers by A/R and by denials.

7. Staffing and coverage

  • ☐ More than one person can do each critical billing task.
  • ☐ Billing staff have protected time (they aren't constantly pulled to the front desk).
  • ☐ Procedures for each queue are documented.
  • ☐ We have a plan for vacations, leave and turnover.
  • ☐ We've compared our fully loaded in-house cost with other options (see worksheet).

8. HIPAA safeguards for vendors and remote staff

  • ☐ Every vendor or agency with PHI access has a signed BAA.
  • ☐ Every user has a unique login with multi-factor authentication.
  • ☐ Remote access uses a VPN or virtual desktop; local downloads are blocked.
  • ☐ Access is role-based and removed the same day someone leaves.
  • ☐ Our HIPAA risk analysis includes remote and offshore access.
  • ☐ Vendors must notify us of incidents within a defined timeframe.

Scoring

  • 0-5 "No/Not sure": solid foundation; focus on fine-tuning.
  • 6-15: specific gaps; pick the section with the most "No" answers and fix it first.
  • 16+: the revenue cycle likely needs more structure or capacity. Consider a free staffing assessment.

(Scoring bands are a simple rule of thumb, not a validated benchmark.)
