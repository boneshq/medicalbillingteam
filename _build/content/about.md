# About this site

Medical Billing Team is a resource for US medical practices and billing companies that are sorting out how to staff their billing and revenue cycle work. We write plain-English guides on topics like denial management, A/R follow-up, eligibility and prior authorization, HIPAA safeguards for outsourced staff and the real costs of different staffing models.

## Disclosure: who operates this site

**This site is operated by Alliance Global Solutions, a provider of remote medical billing and revenue cycle staff. We may contact you about our services if you request it.**

That means we have a commercial interest in the topics we write about. Alliance Global Solutions provides remote staff, based in the Philippines, for roles such as billing specialists, A/R follow-up, eligibility and insurance verification (VOB), prior authorization, payment posting, denial follow-up and admissions or intake support. You can learn more about the company on its own website: [about Alliance Global Solutions](https://www.allianceglobalsolutions.com/about-us/).

We think you should know that before you read anything here, so this disclosure also appears in the footer of every page and next to our forms.

## How we write our guides

- **We explain options fairly**, including when in-house staff or a full-service billing company is a better fit than remote staff.
- **We cite public sources.** Any statistic on this site links to a public source such as CMS, HHS, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the AMA or KFF. Where we cite a vendor survey, we say so. If we can't find a reliable public source for a number, we leave the number out.
- **We don't promise results.** Billing outcomes depend on your payers, specialty, documentation and processes. We don't claim that any staffing model will cut denials or A/R days by a set amount.
- **No fake proof.** You won't find invented testimonials, star ratings, client logos or case studies here. If we ever publish a client story, it will be real, permission-based and clearly labeled.
- **We are not "HIPAA certified," and neither is anyone else.** There is no official HIPAA certification. We explain what safeguards to look for in our [HIPAA guide](/hipaa-outsourced-medical-billing/).
- **We review and update.** Each guide shows a "last reviewed" date. Billing rules change, and we update pages when CMS, payers or code sets change.

## Not legal or financial advice

Our content is general information. It is not legal, compliance, coding or financial advice. For decisions about HIPAA, contracts or reimbursement, talk to qualified advisers.
