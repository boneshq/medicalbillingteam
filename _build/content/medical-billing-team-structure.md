# Medical billing team structure: a documented staffing example

*By the Medical Billing Team editors · Published November 9, 2026 · Updated November 9, 2026 · [How we research and review](/about/#how-we-write-our-guides)*

**Short answer:** Structure a medical billing team around distinct work queues and the decisions each role can make. One reviewed Alliance Global Solutions customer invoice documents VOB, medical collections, authorization and appeal resolution, and medical billing specialists on the same staffing schedule. That is evidence of an actual mix of billed roles. It does not establish the right headcount for your business or prove a collection result.

Medical Billing Team is operated by AGS. This guide separates the documented example from a small-team budget you can adapt before [requesting a staffing proposal](/contact/).

## What one customer staffing schedule documents

Our editors reviewed an AGS-issued customer invoice with an attached staffing schedule covering **October 1–15, 2026**. The following specialist designations appeared together on that schedule. Each had a listed base rate of **$8.50 per hour** during that period.

| Designation on the schedule | What the record establishes |
|---|---|
| VOB Specialist | This role was included in the customer's billed staffing |
| Medical Collections Specialist | This role was included in the same schedule |
| Authorization and Appeal Resolution Specialist | This role was included in the same schedule |
| Medical Billing Specialist | This role was included in the same schedule |

Client identity, exact staffing counts and individual hours are withheld. These are selected specialist categories, not a statement of the customer's total workforce. The schedule also does not show the customer's task instructions, claim volume, collections, denial rate or reasons for choosing that mix. It cannot establish a before-and-after result or a recommended staffing ratio.

The useful takeaway is narrower: **an outsourced engagement can contain several named specialist roles, rather than treating every assignment as a generic biller position.** Your scope still needs to define what those titles mean in your operation. For example, do not assume a medical collections designation covers every A/R or denial task.

The broader five-invoice review found medical billing specialist base rates of **$8.50–$12.64 per hour**. These are historical customer-billed components, not employee take-home pay, a market average or a current offer. See the [pricing methodology and staffing estimator](/pricing/) for the sample limits and other supported roles.

## Turn the role list into a work plan

For your own team, name the queue before assigning the title. These are questions to resolve in a staffing discussion, not claims about the anonymous customer's workflow.

| Work to scope | Define the handoff before hiring |
|---|---|
| Eligibility and benefits verification | What information must be recorded, what remains unresolved and who decides the next action |
| Medical collections follow-up | Which accounts and payers are assigned, how actions are documented and which decisions require approval |
| Authorization and appeal support | Who supplies supporting information, who approves submissions and who handles clinical questions |
| Medical billing work | Which tasks are included, which systems are used and who reviews exceptions |

Keep clinical decisions and any financial approval outside the role's agreed authority with the responsible person on your team. Use the [RFP template and scorecard](/medical-billing-rfp-template/) to record those boundaries. If you bill for other organizations, also define the account ownership and communication rules described in our [staffing guide for medical billing companies](/medical-billing-company-staffing/).

## A separate three-person planning budget

**Illustration only:** Suppose you want to budget for one VOB specialist, one medical collections specialist and one medical billing specialist, each at 40 hours per week. This is not the anonymous customer's team, a recommended starting size or an availability promise.

| Illustrative role | People | Historical base rate used | Modeled monthly base |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| VOB specialist | 1 | $8.50/hour | $1,473.33 |
| Medical collections specialist | 1 | $8.50/hour | $1,473.33 |
| Medical billing specialist | 1 | $8.50–$12.64/hour | $1,473.33–$2,190.93 |
| **Illustrative team total** | **3** | **Before other charges** | **$4,420.00–$5,137.60** |

Formula: hourly rate × people × 40 hours/week × 52 weeks ÷ 12 months. Totals are calculated before rounding. This converts a weekly staffing assumption into an average-month planning figure; it is not a monthly retainer or the amount on the reviewed invoice.

Overtime and other charges are excluded. Current rates, minimum commitments, coverage, software, onboarding and supervision costs require a written proposal. Adjust your assumptions with the [role-based estimator](/pricing/); do not scale headcount from the example without reviewing actual workload.

## Agree on onboarding responsibilities and inclusions

The [AGS medical billing service description](https://www.allianceglobalsolutions.com/medical-billing/), checked November 9, 2026, assigns recruiting and monitoring to AGS and client-specific training to the client. It describes gathering the workload, hiring for the roles, signing agreements and then training and monitoring. AGS also states that it signs a BAA before staff access PHI.

That service description is not an itemized quote. Use it to begin the responsibility discussion:

| Topic | Put this in the written proposal or operating brief |
|---|---|
| Hiring and readiness | Required experience, screening, selection process and what must be completed before work begins |
| Client training | Named trainer, procedures, practice exercises and how readiness will be checked |
| Monitoring and quality | Reporting owner, review frequency, error correction and escalation contact |
| Systems and access | Approved tools, permissions, licenses, setup responsibilities and removal of access |
| Commercial inclusions | Which fees cover recruiting, equipment, monitoring, onboarding or support; which costs remain yours |
| Coverage and changes | Agreed hours, leave coverage, replacement terms, changes in workload and notice requirements |

Neither an invoice rate nor a role title answers all of those questions. Confirm the terms for your engagement. Our [overview of how dedicated staffing works](/how-it-works/) can help you prepare for that conversation.

## Check the first assignment before expanding

Choose a defined queue and record its opening size, incoming work, completed actions, blocked items and the quality-review method. Those measures help distinguish additional capacity from a change in payer behavior or work arriving from your own team. Agree on the reporting definitions and review checkpoints before using results to justify another role.

To discuss your team, [submit your business contact details](/contact/). For the follow-up conversation, prepare the roles, approximate workload, systems, required hours and training owner. Share aggregate business information only; do not send patient records or claim files through this website.

## FAQ

### Is the staffing example based on a real customer?

Yes. The four role categories appeared together in one AGS customer's staffing schedule for October 1–15, 2026. We withhold the customer's identity and exact staffing counts. The record documents billed staffing, not collection results, total client headcount or a recommended staffing ratio.

### Is the three-person budget the customer's actual team?

No. It is a separate planning example with one VOB specialist, one medical collections specialist and one medical billing specialist working 40 hours per week each. Historical observed rates produce a modeled monthly base of $4,420.00–$5,137.60 before overtime and other charges.

### How many people should our medical billing team have?

Start with the assigned queues, incoming volume, handling time, coverage hours and work that needs experienced review. Role titles or another customer's staffing pattern cannot establish your required headcount. Ask AGS to scope a team against your workload and confirm current availability and terms.

### Who trains an outsourced AGS team?

AGS's published service model assigns hiring and monitoring to AGS and training on the client's systems, payers and rules to the client. Define the training owner, quality checks, escalation process and related costs in the proposal before starting.
