# How a remote medical billing team works

*By the Medical Billing Team editors · Published October 18, 2026 · Updated November 9, 2026 · [How we research and review](/about/#how-we-write-our-guides)*

**Short answer:** You define the billing work and train the team on your systems, payers and rules. Alliance Global Solutions recruits, screens and monitors dedicated staff. AGS states that it signs a business associate agreement with each client before staff access protected health information. Confirm the staffing, price, access requirements and onboarding schedule in your proposal.

This guide follows [AGS's published medical billing service process](https://www.allianceglobalsolutions.com/medical-billing/), reviewed November 9, 2026. Medical Billing Team is operated by AGS. The model serves medical billing companies, practices and treatment centers that want dedicated staff working within their own processes.

## Step 1: Tell us about your workload

Use the [short form on the home page](/#assessment) or the [contact form](/contact/) to describe your organization, the roles you need and your current billing setup. **Share business-level information only. Never include patient information.**

Prepare the queues you want staffed, approximate workload, systems, payers, coverage hours and the person who will train the team. A billing company can also identify which client accounts or work queues the proposed staff would support, without sending patient records.

## Step 2: Scope the team and review the proposal

Discuss the work you want assigned and the responsibilities your company will retain:

- which queues are falling behind (eligibility, authorizations, claims, denials, A/R, posting);
- your systems, payers and specialty-specific rules;
- your approved method for remote access;
- the hours and coverage you need; and
- who trains staff, reviews work and handles exceptions.

Use the [staffing cost estimator](/pricing/) for an initial budget based on historical invoiced rates. It is not a current quote. Before accepting a proposal, confirm:

- **Staffing minimums:** How many people and hours are required for the role?
- **Total cost:** What is the current rate, and are recruiting, supervision, equipment, software, training, overtime or other charges included or billed separately?
- **Selection and timing:** Can you interview candidates, who makes the selection, and what start date can AGS commit to?
- **Working arrangements:** What reporting, quality checks, absence coverage, replacement process and notice terms apply?
- **Access:** Which systems and permissions are approved, and who sets up and manages access?

These are proposal questions, not published promises that every arrangement includes the same services or terms. The [documented team-structure example](/medical-billing-team-structure/) can help you think through role boundaries without treating another customer's team as a staffing requirement.

## Step 3: Recruit, complete agreements and train

If you decide to go ahead:

1. **AGS recruits and screens staff** for the roles you described.
2. **Complete the agreements.** AGS states that it signs a **business associate agreement (BAA)** with each client before its staff access protected health information.
3. **Prepare the agreed access and training.** Your company trains the team on its systems, payers, procedures and escalation rules. Confirm the access setup with the people responsible for your systems.
4. **Begin the agreed work.** AGS handles monitoring and support. Establish the training plan, work review and reporting expectations before the start date.

Recruiting and onboarding timing depends on the arrangement. Ask for a proposed schedule and the dependencies your company needs to complete.

## Day to day

AGS's published model assigns hiring and monitoring to AGS and training on your processes to your company. Turn that division into a practical operating plan:

- Name the person who sets priorities and answers staff questions.
- Define which decisions staff can make and which require escalation.
- Agree on how work is reviewed, how exceptions are tracked and when reporting is due.
- Confirm how changes in roles, hours or headcount are handled under your agreement.

For ideas on measurement, see [RCM KPIs, defined](/revenue-cycle-management-staffing/). A staffing arrangement does not guarantee a collection result or a specific change in denial rates.

## FAQ

### Do remote staff use our software or theirs?

AGS describes a client-led model: you train dedicated staff on your systems, payers and rules. Confirm the software, approved access method and permissions for each role before onboarding.

### Who manages the remote staff day to day?

AGS hires and monitors the staff; you train them on your workflows. Agree on daily priorities, the client-side supervisor, reporting, quality checks and escalation responsibilities in the proposal.

### What do we need to have in place first?

Prepare the tasks, systems, coverage needs and training owner for each role. AGS states that it signs a business associate agreement with each client before staff access protected health information. Confirm the access requirements and remaining onboarding steps with AGS before starting.

### Can we start with one person?

Ask AGS to confirm its current minimum staffing and hours requirements for your role. Its published examples describe teams ranging from two or three reps to more than 50; those examples do not establish a contractual minimum or guarantee single-person availability.
