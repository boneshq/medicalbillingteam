# How a remote medical billing team works

A dedicated remote billing team is people, not software. Here is what the process looks like from the first conversation to day-to-day work.

## Step 1: Tell us about your workload

Use the [short form on the home page](/#assessment) or the [longer contact form](/contact/). We ask for practice-level details only: specialty, number of providers, a collections range and your current setup. **Never include patient information.**

## Step 2: Free staffing assessment

A billing staffing specialist talks with you, usually for 20 to 30 minutes, about:

- which queues are falling behind (eligibility, authorizations, claims, denials, A/R, posting);
- your systems, payers and specialty-specific rules;
- whether your systems can support secure remote access;
- the hours and coverage you need; and
- whether a remote team is a sensible fit at all.

If it isn't, for example because your processes are undocumented or your payer contracts restrict offshore access, we'll tell you.

## Step 3: Meet candidates, sign agreements, onboard

If you decide to go ahead:

1. **Role definition.** We agree on tasks, hours, systems and how performance will be measured.
2. **Candidate interviews.** You interview shortlisted candidates and choose who joins your team.
3. **Agreements.** A services agreement and a **business associate agreement (BAA)** are signed before anyone receives access to protected health information. See our [HIPAA guide](/hipaa-outsourced-medical-billing/).
4. **Secure access setup.** Unique logins, multi-factor authentication, role-based permissions and secure remote access (VDI or VPN), with no local downloads.
5. **Training on your workflows.** Your procedures, payers, escalation rules and reporting.
6. **Ramp-up.** Start with a defined queue, review work closely in the first weeks, then expand.

## Day to day

- Staff work your queues during agreed hours, often overlapping US business hours.
- You set priorities and approve anything outside the agreed rules (write-offs, refunds, appeals strategy).
- You get regular reporting on volumes worked and the KPIs you choose, such as days in A/R or denial rate. See [RCM KPIs, defined](/revenue-cycle-management-staffing/).
- You can add roles or reduce capacity under the terms of your agreement.

## What we don't do

- We don't ask for patient information through this website.
- We don't claim to be "HIPAA certified"; no such official certification exists.
- We don't promise specific outcomes before understanding your data.
