# How we vet vendors

Before a vendor appears in our directory, we ask the same questions our [HIPAA guide](/hipaa-outsourced-medical-billing/) tells you to ask any billing staffing agency.

## What we ask of every listed vendor

- A signed business associate agreement before any PHI access
- Access limited to the minimum necessary for each role
- Unique logins, MFA and secure remote access
- HIPAA training for every staff member who touches your systems
- Written breach-notification terms
- A clear statement of who employs, manages and backfills the staff

## Straight answers about how this works

This site is operated by Alliance Global Solutions. Alliance Global Solutions is currently the only vendor in our directory, and we are affiliated with it. When we add other vendors, they will go through the same checks.
