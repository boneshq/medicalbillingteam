# Outsourced medical billing staffing prices and team cost estimator

*By the Medical Billing Team editors · Published November 7, 2026 · Updated November 7, 2026 · [How we research and review](/about/#how-we-write-our-guides)*

**Short answer:** Reviewed AGS invoices for October 1–15, 2026 show medical billing base rates of **$8.50–$12.64 per hour**. At 40 hours per week, that models **$1,473.33–$2,190.93 per person per average month**, before overtime and other charges. These are historical observations from a limited sample, not a current quote. Build your budget from the roles, hours and headcount your team needs. Use the estimator below to explore a planning range, then [request a written AGS quote](#footer-cta) for your actual requirements. Dedicated staffing prices cover a different responsibility from a full-service billing company's percentage-of-collections fee.

## Choose the work before choosing the role

A title alone can hide different workloads. Specify the queue, systems, required experience, coverage hours and who will review the work. That makes the estimate more useful when you speak with AGS.

| If you need help with… | Describe this in your quote request |
|---|---|
| Billing and claim processing | Included tasks, expected volume, systems and exception handling |
| [A/R follow-up](/ar-specialist/) | Age bands, payer mix, follow-up expectations and documentation rules |
| [Denial work](/denial-management/) | Common reasons, correction versus appeal scope and approval authority |
| [Eligibility or VOB](/eligibility-verification-prior-authorization/) | Required checks, coverage schedule and how unanswered questions are escalated |
| [Prior authorization](/prior-authorization-specialist/) | Submission, tracking and follow-up scope, plus clinical escalation ownership |
| Front-office support | Call, scheduling or administrative tasks and hours of coverage |

These are examples to discuss, not interchangeable pricing categories. The staffing team must confirm the proposed role, candidate requirements and service scope. For a billing company hiring across multiple queues, see [outsourced staff for medical billing companies](/medical-billing-company-staffing/).

## Read the estimate correctly

Keep the billing unit visible. An hourly rate, a weekly invoice total and a monthly staffing fee cannot be compared without the hours, number of staff and covered period.

- **Hourly planning cost:** the selected rate multiplied by billable hours and headcount.
- **Monthly planning cost:** use the monthly conversion stated beside the estimate. It is an average-budget assumption, not a promise that each month's bill is identical.
- **Mixed teams:** calculate each role separately, then add the role totals.
- **Your full operating budget:** add the costs your organization retains after checking the proposal.

Do not treat a staff member's pay as the client service price. For a domestic-hiring comparison, the [state wage and benefit calculator](/medical-billing-staffing-cost-by-state/) provides a separate in-house baseline. Its BLS wage figures are not AGS rates.

## What can change your final price?

The written proposal should confirm the role requirements and schedule, what AGS will provide, what your team will provide, and the commercial terms. Use this checklist to make any differences from the estimate explicit.

| Item to confirm | What to ask |
|---|---|
| Role and experience | Which tasks and skills does the quoted position cover? |
| Hours and coverage | What counts as billable time, and how are holidays or extra hours handled? |
| Tools and access | Who supplies and pays for each required system or seat? |
| Training and oversight | What support is included, and what time must our managers provide? |
| Continuity | What are the leave, replacement and coverage arrangements? |
| Term and changes | What minimum commitment, notice period or adjustment process applies? |
| Other charges | Are setup, taxes, reimbursements or other agreed charges applicable? |

## FAQ

### Is the staffing estimator a quote from AGS?

No. It is a planning estimate for the roles and hours you select. Final pricing requires a written AGS proposal that confirms role requirements, schedule, headcount, included services and commercial terms.

### Why can two billing roles have different prices?

Required experience, task complexity, schedule, supervision and engagement terms can differ. Define the work and required skills before comparing prices, and ask which assumptions the written quote uses.

### Does a monthly estimate mean every month will have the same invoice?

No. A monthly planning conversion is not an invoice schedule. Actual charges depend on the agreement and applicable billable hours, adjustments and other agreed charges. Check the estimator's displayed assumptions and the final proposal.

### What other costs should we budget for?

Confirm responsibility for software and clearinghouse seats, secure access, equipment, onboarding, training, quality review and management time. Also ask about overtime, holidays, coverage, minimum commitments and exit terms. Do not assume these are included or charged separately without the quote.

### Is this the same as percentage-of-collections medical billing?

No. This page estimates the cost of dedicated staff. A managed billing service may price an agreed function as a percentage of collections or under another fee model. Compare scope and responsibility as well as the number.
