# Frequently asked questions

## About remote billing staff

**What is a remote medical billing team?**
Dedicated billing and revenue cycle staff who work remotely in your systems, under your workflows. You direct the work; a staffing provider recruits, employs and supports the staff. [How it works →](/how-it-works/)

**How is a staffing model different from outsourcing to a billing company?**
A billing company usually takes over the function, often for a percentage of collections. With staffing, you get dedicated people in your software and keep control. [Cost comparison →](/medical-billing-staffing-cost/)

**What roles can remote staff cover?**
Billing and claims, A/R follow-up, denial follow-up, eligibility/VOB, prior authorization, payment posting, coding (with credentials), credentialing and admissions or intake support. [RCM staffing guide →](/revenue-cycle-management-staffing/)

**Do you work with medical billing companies?**
Yes. The staffing provider usually acts as a subcontractor business associate and signs a BAA with the billing company.

## HIPAA and security

**Is anyone "HIPAA certified"?**
No. There is no official HIPAA certification, and HHS's Office for Civil Rights doesn't certify vendors ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-information-technology/cloud-computing/index.html)). Ask about specific safeguards. [HIPAA guide →](/hipaa-outsourced-medical-billing/)

**Will the staffing provider sign a BAA?**
A provider whose staff access PHI on your behalf is generally a business associate ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html)), and a BAA should be signed before any access.

**Is offshore billing allowed under HIPAA?**
HIPAA has no offshore ban, but HHS notes added risk and enforceability concerns. Check payer and Medicaid contracts too.
