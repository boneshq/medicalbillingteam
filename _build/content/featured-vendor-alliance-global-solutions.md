# Featured vendor: Alliance Global Solutions

*By the Medical Billing Team editors · Published October 18, 2026 · Updated October 18, 2026 · [How we research and review](/about/#how-we-write-our-guides)*

**Short answer:** Alliance Global Solutions provides dedicated remote staff for medical billing and revenue cycle work, with staff in the Philippines and Colombia. It hires and monitors the staff, its clients train them on their workflows, and it signs a business associate agreement with each client before any PHI access. It specializes in medical billing, including verification of benefits (VOB) for drug and alcohol treatment centers and broader RCM. It also operates this site, so read this profile as an affiliated listing.

- **What it provides:** dedicated remote employees for medical billing and revenue cycle work
- **Where staff are based:** the Philippines and Colombia

## How the model works

Alliance Global Solutions places dedicated employees with each client. They aren't a shared pool that rotates between accounts. The company handles hiring and monitoring; the client trains the staff on its own payers, software and rules, then directs the day-to-day work. That keeps the client in control of the process and its payer relationships, which is the main difference from a full-service billing company. See [medical billing outsourcing: three models](/medical-billing-outsourcing/) for how this compares.

Team size depends on the client, from 2-3 dedicated staff to 50+. A small outpatient center might start with a few people on verification and follow-up; a billing company serving many practices might use a larger team across several queues, and Alliance Global Solutions handles a lot of back-end work for clients like that. Some client relationships have lasted five to six years.

### Where are Alliance Global Solutions staff located?

Its staff are based in the Philippines and Colombia.

Yes. Alliance Global Solutions signs a business associate agreement with each client before any of its staff access protected health information. It does not claim to be HIPAA certified, because no official certification exists.

Clients range from small outpatient centers to large billing companies that handle billing for multiple clients, with teams from 2-3 dedicated staff to 50+. Some client relationships have lasted five to six years. References are available on request; we don't list current clients.
