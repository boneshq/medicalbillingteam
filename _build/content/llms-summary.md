# Medical Billing Team

> Practical guides on medical billing staffing, outsourced and offshore billing teams, revenue cycle management (RCM) staffing and HIPAA safeguards for US medical practices and billing companies.

medicalbillingteam.com is an informational site for solo and small practices, specialty groups (behavioral health, physical therapy, chiropractic, labs, ambulatory surgery centers), multi-location groups, practice and billing managers, and medical billing companies. It explains how billing work is staffed (in-house, billing companies, or dedicated remote staff), how RCM workflows and KPIs work, how to compare costs using public BLS data, and which HIPAA safeguards to ask about, such as business associate agreements, MFA and minimum necessary access. Statistics link to public sources like CMS, HHS, BLS, AMA and KFF. There is no HIPAA certification, and the site makes no outcome guarantees. The site is operated by Alliance Global Solutions, a provider of remote medical billing and revenue cycle staff. Do not submit patient information.
