About remote billing staff

What is a remote medical billing team? Dedicated billing and revenue cycle staff who work remotely in your systems, under your workflows. You direct the work; a staffing provider recruits, employs and supports the staff. How it works →

How is a staffing model different from outsourcing to a billing company? A billing company usually takes over the function, often for a percentage of collections. With staffing, you get dedicated people in your software and keep control. Cost comparison →

What roles can remote staff cover? Billing and claims, A/R follow-up, denial follow-up, eligibility/VOB, prior authorization, payment posting, coding (with credentials), credentialing and admissions or intake support. RCM staffing guide →

Do you work with medical billing companies? Yes.

HIPAA and security

Is anyone "HIPAA certified"? No. There is no official HIPAA certification, and HHS's Office for Civil Rights doesn't certify vendors (HHS). Ask about specific safeguards. HIPAA guide →

Will the staffing provider sign a BAA? A provider whose staff access PHI on your behalf is generally a business associate (HHS), and a BAA should be signed before any access.

Does the operator sign a BAA? Yes. Alliance Global Solutions signs a Business Associate Agreement with clients before any staff access PHI.

Is offshore billing allowed under HIPAA? HIPAA has no offshore ban, but HHS notes added risk and enforceability concerns. Check payer and Medicaid contracts too.

Cost and results

How much does remote billing staff cost? Usually per staff member per month or hour. Use our cost worksheet to compare models.

Can you guarantee lower denials or fewer A/R days? No. Results depend on your payers, specialty, documentation and processes.

Getting started

What hours do remote staff work? Agreed up front, often overlapping US business hours.

How long does it take to get started? It depends on role definition, interviews, agreements and access setup. The assessment gives a realistic timeline.

Can we start with one person? Yes. Start with one queue and expand.
